# INDIA-AI-001 — IndiaAI Innovation Challenge 2026

Status: Problem Extraction / Deep Validation candidate

## Selected sub-case

**INDIA-AI-001A — MSME Virtual Negotiation Assistance**

### One-line problem
Indian micro and small enterprises face delayed-payment disputes whose resolution is slow, costly, and administratively complex; the Ministry is seeking to improve the existing digital dispute-resolution pathway with AI-enabled negotiation assistance.

## Deeper problem hypothesis — NOT YET OWNER-DECLARED

A potentially deeper systemic issue is that the transaction and dispute-resolution architecture does not sufficiently prevent or rapidly resolve payment friction between MSMEs and buyers. A future concept could therefore address **transaction architecture, commitment mechanisms, negotiation norms, and dispute prevention**, rather than only adding an AI assistant after a dispute occurs.

This hypothesis must not be treated as the official problem statement until owner evidence confirms that the Ministry is seeking such a broader intervention.

## Evidence status

- Official IndiaAI challenge: Problem Statement 1 explicitly requests an AI-enabled virtual negotiation assistant for MSME digital dispute resolution.
- Ministry of MSME already operates an Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) framework and MSME Samadhaan.
- Official ODR guidance states that the existing Samadhaan portal functions primarily as case tracker/e-filing, while the remaining dispute-resolution process requires manual intervention and can be costly and time-consuming.
- The ODR framework already includes Digital Guided Pathway, unmanned negotiation, mediation and arbitration options.
- Therefore the discovery question is no longer whether a digital dispute-resolution problem exists; it is whether the remaining friction is fundamentally a **transaction-system / dispute-system design problem** that warrants a broader method or concept.

## Validation gates still open

- G2 Problem Owner: Ministry of MSME — supported at challenge level.
- G3 Owner-Declared: AI negotiation assistance is owner-declared; broader transaction-culture hypothesis is not.
- G4 Proof of Demand: needs quantitative case-volume, delay and settlement evidence.
- G5 Committed Capital: government ODR programme exists; quantify committed expenditure and deployment scope.
- G6 Target Capacity: quantify intended resolution capacity and service throughput.
- G7 Operational Delay: quantify current resolution timeline and manual-work burden.
- G8 Economic/strategic loss: quantify working-capital and business-opportunity impact.
- G10 Intervention-Seeking: confirmed for the AI negotiation-assistance scope; broader systemic intervention remains unconfirmed.
- G11 Economic threshold: not yet established.

## Decision

Keep INDIA-AI-001A active, but investigate the **deeper transaction/dispute architecture hypothesis** before treating the case as a generic AI product opportunity.
