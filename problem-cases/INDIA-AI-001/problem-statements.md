# INDIA-AI-001 — Problem Statements

## Source status

Primary source: IndiaAI / AIKosh — IndiaAI Innovation Challenge 2026 (Ministry of AYUSH / Ministry of MSME). The official competition page states that the challenge seeks market-ready AI solutions for high-impact public-sector challenges and intends to identify, pilot and scale solutions within government systems.

## Problem Statement 1 — AI Enabled Virtual Negotiation Assistance

**Owner:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)

**Underlying problem:** MSMEs frequently face delayed payments from buyers due to administrative, procedural and operational complexities. Conventional dispute resolution is resource-intensive in time and cost.

**Existing intervention:** MSME has developed an Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal with an optional pre-MSEFC digital settlement pathway.

**Requested intervention:** An AI/ML-powered virtual negotiation assistant to improve accessibility, reduce resolution timelines and improve dispute-handling efficiency.

**Indicative functions:** voice-based intake/form population; document analysis; dispute classification and missing-document detection; outcome prediction using statutory provisions and precedents; multilingual real-time negotiation support; settlement drafting.

**Method/Concept relevance:** HIGH. The challenge is framed as an integrated decision/workflow system rather than a single model. Potential methodological contribution includes dispute-process architecture, negotiation logic, human-AI decision boundaries, evidence/precedent reasoning, multilingual interaction, escalation and settlement workflow design.

**Current project assessment:** Highest-priority subcase for further validation.

## Problem Statement 2 — AI-powered MSE Agent Mapping Tool

**Owner:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)

**Underlying problem:** MSEs struggle with buyer discovery and market outreach; the TEAM onboarding workflow involves manual data entry, inconsistent product categorisation and labour-intensive verification, causing onboarding delays and application rejection.

**Requested intervention:** An AI-driven intelligent MSE agent mapping system to automate, optimise and scale the TEAM process, including intelligent matching of vendors/entities based on attributes, capabilities and operational requirements.

**Method/Concept relevance:** HIGH. Strong systems-architecture and organisational-workflow component: ontology/classification, matching logic, verification, human-in-the-loop governance, exception handling and scalable onboarding architecture.

**Current project assessment:** Second-priority subcase; requires deeper evidence on operational scale, owner pain, delay economics and intervention demand.

## Problem Statement 3 — Intelligent AYUSH Health Systems

**Owner:** Ministry of AYUSH

**Underlying problem:** Need for AI-enabled use of large-scale historical health data for disease-trend detection, public-health risk forecasting and personalised treatment/lifestyle recommendations.

**Method/Concept relevance:** MEDIUM. There is substantial systems and governance complexity, but the domain is health/clinical and therefore requires specialist clinical validation and carries higher safety constraints. It is less directly aligned with the user's strongest problem-solving/organisational architecture positioning than the two MSME cases.

## Screening decision

The challenge is **not** treated as one validated problem. It contains at least three distinct problem statements and must be decomposed accordingly.

Priority for IMS2050 investigation:

1. **INDIA-AI-001A — MSME Virtual Negotiation Assistance** — investigate first.
2. **INDIA-AI-001B — MSME Intelligent Agent Mapping** — investigate second.
3. **INDIA-AI-001C — Intelligent AYUSH Health Systems** — retain as secondary unless stronger fit/evidence emerges.

Important: challenge participation status and problem validation status are separate. The official competition timeline shows applications opened 15 January 2026 and closed 2 March 2026; therefore the current work is evidence-based problem discovery/validation, not an application attempt.
